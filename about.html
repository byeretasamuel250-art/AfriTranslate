About Us

Afrischool is a translation app built to help people learn and communicate across African languages. It supports text and voice translation, so you can type or speak in one language and get it in another.

Who we are

Afrischool is built and operated by BYERETA-TECH, a small technology team based in Uganda.

What we offer

  • Text translation between English and several African languages.
  • Voice input and text-to-speech, so you can listen to translations as well as read them.
  • A subscription plan for continued access to premium features, paid securely through Pesapal.
