About Us

Afri.school is an online school built to help Ugandans to learn and communicate across their languages including Swahili and Kinyarwanda, so you can type or speak in one language and get it in another.

Who we are

Afri.school is built and operated by BYERETA SAMUEL ENTERPRISES, a technology team based in Uganda.

What we offer

  • Text/Voice translation between several African languages directly on App.
  • Online classes with your language Teachers.
  • Phyisical classes for exams
