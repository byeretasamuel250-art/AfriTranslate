About Afrischool

Afrischool helps people translate text and speech between English and African languages, including Luganda, Swahili, Kinyarwanda, Acholi, Alur, and Runyankole/Rukiga, with more languages being added over time.

Who we are

Afrischool is operated by Afrischool, under BYERETA-TECH, based in Uganda.

How it works

Translations are powered by Sunbird AI, a research organisation focused on AI for African languages, with an automatic quality cross-check against Google's translation service where available. Voice input and text-to-speech let you speak or listen instead of typing and reading.
