About Us

Afri.school is an online school built to help Ugandans and Africans learn and communicate across their languages, including Luganda, Swahili, Kinyarwanda, and other African languages. Type or speak in one language and get it translated into another — instantly, right in the app.

Why Afri.school

Many African languages are spoken every day but rarely taught formally. Afri.school was created to close that gap — helping people reconnect with their mother tongue, communicate across language barriers within their own country or region, and pass their language on to the next generation.

Who we are

Afri.school is built and operated by BYERETA SAMUEL ENTERPRISES, a technology team based in Uganda focused on building practical tools for African communities.

What we offer

  • Text and voice translation between several African languages, directly in the app.
  • Online classes with real language teachers.
  • Physical classes for learners preparing for exams.
  • Support for languages including Luganda, Swahili, Kinyarwanda, and more.
