Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 September 2026

Afrischool ("the app", "we", "us") is operated by Afrischool, under BYERETA-TECH. This policy explains what information we collect when you use the app, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. If you have questions, contact us at byeretasamuel250@gmail.com.

1. Information we collect

WhatWhy
NameUsed as your sign-in identifier and shown back to you in the app.
Email addressUsed only for password recovery and important account notices - never shown to other users, and never used for marketing without your separate consent.
Phone numberUsed for account contact purposes (e.g. class or update notifications).
PasswordStored in hashed (encrypted, non-readable) form by our authentication provider, Supabase - we never see or store your plain-text password.
Text you translateSent to Sunbird AI (and, for some language pairs, Google's translation service) to produce the translation, and cached to avoid repeat processing costs.
Voice recordingsSent to Sunbird AI's speech-to-text service if you use voice input, to transcribe what you said.
Payment recordsThe amount, date, and status of a payment attempt (not your card or mobile money details, which Pesapal handles directly).
Technical/session dataIP address and session identifiers, used for rate-limiting and to enforce single-device sign-in.

2. Third-party services we use

  • Supabase - hosts our database and handles account authentication.
  • Sunbird AI - provides translation, speech-to-text, and text-to-speech processing.
  • Google Cloud Translation API - used to cross-check translation quality for supported language pairs; the text you're translating is sent to Google for this purpose.
  • Pesapal - processes payments via mobile money and card. We never receive your full card number or mobile money PIN.
  • Vercel - hosts the app itself.

Each of these providers processes data under their own privacy terms in addition to this policy. We only share the minimum information each service needs to do its job (for example, Pesapal only receives your name/email and payment amount, never your translation history).

3. What we don't do

  • We don't sell your personal data to anyone.
  • We don't show your email or phone number to other users.
  • We don't use your translated text or voice recordings to train our own AI models.

4. Data retention

We keep your account information for as long as your account is active. Translated text and audio-generation results may be cached and shared anonymously across users purely to avoid repeat processing (the cache is keyed by the text itself, not by who requested it). If you delete your account, your profile, payment history, and session records are removed; cached translation text (which is not linked to any individual account once cached) may persist as general-purpose cached data.

5. Your rights

You can:

  • Access or correct your account information at any time from within the app.
  • Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated personal data.
  • Withdraw consent for optional communications at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, email byeretasamuel250@gmail.com. We'll respond within a reasonable time, generally within 30 days.

6. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords, and access controls that restrict who inside our organization can view account data. No system is 100% secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security, but we take reasonable, ongoing steps to protect your information.

7. Children's privacy

Afrischool is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under that age. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. We'll update the "Last updated" date above when we do, and for significant changes we'll make a reasonable effort to notify users in-app.

9. Contact

Afrischool is operated by Afrischool, under BYERETA-TECH. For any privacy questions or requests, contact byeretasamuel250@gmail.com.
